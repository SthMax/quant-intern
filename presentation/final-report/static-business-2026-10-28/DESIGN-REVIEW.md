## Overall assessment

This is a coherent working business presentation. The front-, middle- and back-office progression is clear, the current-workflow / industry-example / proposed-application sequence carries the argument, and the strongest pages show a concrete business artifact. The native quote table on slide 11, the client document on slide 15, the operational matrix on slide 22 and the two information flows on slide 30 all give the audience an immediate visual structure. The compact static office locator is sufficiently subordinate on the task pages. Retain these clear compositions as the basis for refinement.

The remaining weaknesses are ambiguous visual meaning in a few places, output emphasis that varies between proposals, and repeated levels of blue bold text that compete for priority. Slide 8 gives its calculation block substantially more prominence than its draft research output. Slide 21 encloses the assessment in a large frame but does not distinguish the assessment, affected fund and follow-up within it. Slides 16–18 use yellow at the field level, while slide 23 uses it for particular additions. That difference makes actual changes harder to identify in the revised client update.

The useful next revision would be selective: fix the two semantic ambiguities below, strengthen the outputs on slides 8 and 21, connect the supporting capabilities on slides 5 and 31, and polish a small set of line breaks and spacing decisions. Many other compositions should be retained. This judgment is based on the frozen draft inspected for this review.

## Cross-deck recommendations

### Preserve the useful structure

Keep the 33-slide order and the seven-task framework. Slides 3 and 29 provide the full maps; task slides can retain their small current-office locator and specific subtitle. The maps, actual documents, native table, alert page, U-shaped development process and implementation phases already provide meaningful variety. Retain variations that reflect these different content functions.

The current static locator works best on slides 4–28, where one office name is active. On 31–33, all three names are grey and no state is communicated. One plain “Across the firm” line would be clearer, while preserving restrained context. Keep locators within the body region and leave the corporate footer zone empty.

### Use a consistent hierarchy within each page

At this 25.40 cm slide width, the current 20.5 pt main titles and roughly 11–13 pt body text are generally readable in the inspected full-slide display. A generic large-slide font minimum would be a poor reason to enlarge everything. The more material problem is that a blue introductory sentence, a blue section heading and a blue concluding claim sometimes receive almost the same emphasis.

Choose one primary body-level focal point per page: the actual output on a proposal, the evidence on an industry example, or the work/problem on a current-workflow page. Then demote supporting method or scope sentences. Slides 11, 15, 19, 22 and 30 already do this well. On 7 and 23, an evidence figure can receive stronger size contrast while its exact scope remains nearby. On 14, the bottom FundGPT scope sentence should be quieter than the draft answer.

Resolve the dense pages by reallocating space on 8 and 21, set manual line breaks on 6/11/14/18/20, and improve group spacing on 18/24/29. Keep long qualifiers out of narrow final lines. Check these particular breaks in PowerPoint because its wrapping can differ from the renderer, as seen on 18.

### Keep color meanings stable and local

Teal Company A evidence and purple client documents are recognizable without detached badges. Add the actual company name within evidence headings on 4 and 8 so color is a reinforcement rather than the only identity cue. Keep people and tools visually subordinate to the business content.

Yellow needs one consistent explanation across the client sequence. A review target and a textual change are different things. One small in-content qualifier can define the V1 focus, while yellow spans in V2 show actual additions or revisions. Keep retained raw-material monitoring unhighlighted when the purpose is to identify additions. Slide 23's precise additions and slide 24's written **Added / Retained** distinction make the business meaning clear without hiding any full passage.

### Let connections communicate specific relationships

Use arrows for a transfer or sequence that the audience can name. Slide 22's row arrows and slide 27's numbered return path are effective. On 8, make the outgoing calculation request and returning result distinct. On 9, keep external actors beside the stages they support. On 5 and 31, a support band or bracket may explain a dependency better than another process arrow. Leave parallel questions, such as slide 19's three investigations, unconnected.

### Retain the business outputs and exact scopes

Keep the full editable V1/V2 passages, native quote table, financial units and explicit LLM contributions. The references reinforce the value of showing actual business evidence. Any larger efficiency callout must retain its reported scope: approximately 50% higher **research preparation efficiency** on 7, and automated data entry for more than 80% of the specified **direct-sales-counter faxes** on 23.

For a live presentation, precise focus cues, balanced lines and the presenter's later animation can guide attention through the complete client passages. For a reading deck, the full text provides useful detail. The user can choose the presentation rhythm while preserving the same evidence.
