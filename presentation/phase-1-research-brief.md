## Proposed narrative from evidence so far

1. **Peer adoption has several forms:** internal platforms, customer-facing
   information services, and procurement. Maturity labels matter.
2. **Source quality is uneven:** detailed AMAC project reports coexist with media
   claims, missing dates, and incomplete vendor disclosure.
3. **The legal analysis needs more than hosting location:** service audience,
   data processing, system importance, third-party role, and internal policy each
   affect the relevant questions.
4. **2026 sources materially change the reference set:** the AMAC LLM standard is
   directly relevant, and SR 26-2 both replaces SR 11-7 and excludes generative/
   agentic AI from scope.
