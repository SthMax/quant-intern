## Enterprise EFundGPT: what is technically disclosed

The company-issued release (FUND-106) is primary because its issuer is explicitly
“E Fund Management” in PR Newswire's “News provided by” and final source fields.
It verifies company-reported DeepSeek use and the EFundGPT upgrade, but gives no
model version, deployment topology or effect size. An AI-themed ETF mentioned
elsewhere in the release is an investment product and adds no adoption evidence.

In his first-person speech, CIO Liu Shuoling describes an internally built
EFundGPT platform and a proprietary training system, with pretraining and LoRA
feeding a knowledge platform. He reports that AI performs one-third of system
development workload on a quarterly measurement basis. The counting method,
quality threshold and costs are absent; this is a management claim, not a measured
one-third reduction in headcount. His future staffing prediction is not an outcome.
[FUND-103](https://gifts.szu.edu.cn/info/1023/1364.htm), section 六.

The AMAC-hosted company account adds a distinct scope indicator: **100+ AIGC
applications**. It does not enumerate them, specify their users, or establish
100 independent production systems. [FUND-102](https://www.amac.org.cn/hydj/djdt/202601/P020260113409049382586.pdf),
PDF p. 8. The list is dated through the association's [publication index](https://www.amac.org.cn/hydj/djdt/).

## Implications for this internship

Research interpretation: the strongest transferable observation is the investment
in shared information and engineering systems around a model. Earnings-commentary
drafting and coding/testing are concrete pilot comparisons to revisit in later
phases; the timing and quality metrics needed for the host organization's assessment still have to
be measured locally. Current public evidence supports questions for architecture
and evaluation design, without supplying internal COD configuration or internal policy.

For next Friday, the primary-evidence slide can show EFundGPT, index integration,
coding/testing and the historical advisory workflow. Place the granular research
Agent volumes and newest external-distribution features in a clearly labelled
verification appendix until their primary records are checked.
