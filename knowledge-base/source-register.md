## Reading and verification rules

- A company-issued release, company-authored original account, technical submission
  or original vendor filing supports its attributable claims, not independent
  validation of operations, control effectiveness or ROI.
- Reporter-mediated articles remain secondary even on a company website/account.
  First-person source identity and original publication must be checked.
  FUND-314 was downgraded after this consistency review.
- FUND-400 is a source-attributed **screening ranking**, not independently
  reconstructed raw AUM data. FUND-401 is older primary AMAC statistics using a
  different measurement method; it is not a current substitute.
- Generic vendor client lists and recruitment requirements do not establish a
  named production application. Do not count source records as deployments.
- Inaccessible sources support only a follow-up locator. Missing dates are not
  inferred from filenames, and current account operators are not projected onto
  historical releases.
- Source/repost chains are not independent corroborations. Direct URL and source
  ID duplicates were checked before integration.
- Exact page/section pointers and human-check status are in linked case notes.
  The original research-batch files retain source-level handoff and review details.
